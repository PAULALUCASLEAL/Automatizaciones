## Direction contract

THESIS: Work is water in a huerta: it arrives scattered, is led through channels and partidores, and reaches each field in order; a gate holds it for a person when something is missing. Refuses the category default of a dark SaaS hero with a floating dashboard and a row of equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Limestone ground #F7F5F1, channel beds in sand #CEC5B8 lined by stone #D8D1C7, surfaces #E8E3DB like whitewashed walls, charcoal #242321 as water/ink and type. Double-line channels, square partidor nodes that round and receive a check when done, small gate bars. Grotesk display (Bricolage Grotesque) with architectural calm; Figtree as the warm, legible body face (owner-confirmed build, amended after review). The hero diagram has a scenario selector: Pedidos y documentos, Captación de clientes, CRM y seguimiento, Atención al cliente; each has its own route, exception branch with a gate bar (or a sales pipeline for CRM) and a detail card. One dark charcoal moment (Cómo trabajamos) and a dark footer.

STORY: Visitor recognises their own repetitive work, sees it routed calmly rather than vanishing, understands four service areas through concrete triggers, checks two demos, and writes to us.
