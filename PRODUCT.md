# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + TypeScript + Vite (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-18). Static build, no backend.

## Users

Owners, managers and operations/administration leads of Spanish SMEs (pymes), initially brands, online shops and distributors of roughly 5–50 people with recurring orders and several disconnected tools. They arrive asking whether someone can take repetitive administrative work off their team without forcing them to change everything.

## Product Purpose

PK Soluciones is an independent, founder-led studio that builds automations, systems integrations, AI-assisted processes and custom software for SMEs. Purpose: "Devolver a las empresas el tiempo y la capacidad de construir su futuro." Success for the website: a visitor understands quickly what is built, recognises their own repetitive work, and starts a conversation through the contact form.

## Positioning

Emotional promise ("Que lo urgente deje espacio a lo importante") backed by concrete, measured operational change: understand the process first, improve one thing, measure before and after. No promise of autonomous perfection or guaranteed savings.

## Operating Context

Typical processes: emailed orders, invoices/documents, customer enquiries, CRM follow-up, reports assembled by hand. Work is routed, validated and reviewed by people at exception points; nothing "magically disappears". Initial market Elche, Alicante, Murcia, plus remote service.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Service areas: administración/documentos/pedidos; atención al cliente y seguimiento; CRM y procesos comerciales; informes y control del negocio. Integrations and custom software span all four.
- Price, compatibility and support always depend on agreed scope.
- Contact form has no delivery endpoint yet; must not fake success.
- Name "PK Soluciones" is provisional (pending domain/trademark checks); keep it in one constant.
- Undecided: founder name, photo, education, experience; legal entity details; contact email; domain.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: PK Soluciones (provisional). Descriptor: "Soluciones y automatización para pymes."
- All visitor copy in natural European Spanish.
- Owner-pinned palette (exact HEX, binding): background #F7F5F1, surface #E8E3DB, mineral sand accent #CEC5B8, secondary text #746B60, primary text/buttons #242321, dark accent/hover #514B44, borders/workflow connections #D8D1C7.
- Feel: calm, warm, architectural, refined. Predominantly light; few intentional dark moments. Sand for surfaces/decoration, never small text. No purple, blue, neon, metallic gold or decorative gradients.
- Workflow visuals: stone connections, light nodes, charcoal pulses; completion via shape, icon and label changes. Original language, not a copy of n8n.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No clients, testimonials, logos, case studies, certifications or quantified results exist and none may be fabricated. Demonstrations use fictional data labelled "Demostración ilustrativa".

## Product Principles

1. Respect the people doing the work; never portray them as inefficient or replaceable.
2. Emotion creates recognition; concrete examples establish credibility.
3. Show work being routed and reviewed, never vanishing.
4. Estimates are labelled as estimates; capacity is not cash.
5. One clear next step: a conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for all text, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion support with meaningful static diagrams, ~44px touch targets, text equivalents for diagrams.
